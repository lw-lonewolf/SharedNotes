
There is a significant amount of difference between software production and industrial level production. Producing even very complex machines in an industrial setting does not allow more than a few thousands modes of operation made possible by the different combinations of the machines settings. On the other hand, a software is capable of having millions of operation possibilities.

There are three major points of comparisons for *industrial product* and *software product* differences: 

##### Product Visibility:
Most industrial level products are *visible*, i.e. flaws in the product can easily be identified and confirmed through visual means, on the other hand a software is invisible effectively making it impossible to predict its flaws. For example, if a door is missing in your new car you will almost instantly recognize this flaw in it, however its harder to do so in a software context, as contents may be missing from the disk or CD from the very start. 
##### Industrial Product Development and Production
###### Product Development:
In this phase the designers and quality assurance (QA) check and test the prototype, in order to find defects 
###### Product Production Planning: 
The part where tools and processes are designed for mass production. Some products even require the construction of a special assembly line etc.
###### Manufacturing: 
At this phase, the production starts and QA procedures are applied to ensure the quality of the output products. This means there are opportunities at every stage and sub-stage to identify problems and faults in the product.
##### Software Product Development and Production
###### Product Development:
During this phase, efforts of development team and the SQA team are directed at finding inherent product defects. At the end of this part a prototype is ready for reproduction.
###### Product Production Planning: 
This phase isn't required in software production process, as manufacturing just normally introduces copies of the code or printing of the user manual.
###### Manufacturing:
Manufacturing of software is limited to copying product and printing copies. 

To conclude, it is pretty apparent that defect detection is harder and more cumbersome in the Software Product Lifecyle/Process 

##### Uniqueness of Software Development Process: 
* *High Complexity, as compared to industrial products*
* *Invisibility of the Software Product*
* *opportunities to detect defects or bugs are limited to product development phase only.*
