Following are the main characteristics of an SQA Environment: 
* *Contractual Conditions:* Finishing requirements within time, budget and timetable constraints
* *Subjection to Customer-Supplier Relationship:* Project team must cooperate continuously with the customer.
* *Required teamwork*
* *Cooperation and coordination with other software teams*
* *Interfaces with other systems*
* *Need to carry on work despite changes in team members*
* *Need to continue carrying out software maintenance for an extended period*
