*Lewis Goldberg* -> Personality can be divided into five basic traits. 
-> Agreeableness (`Friendly`/`Aggressive`)
-> Neuroticism (`Reactive or Unstable Mind`/ `Stable Mind and Calm`)
-> Conscientiousness (`Order and Reliable/Disorder and Unreliable`)
-> Extraversion (`Outgoing or Social`/`Introvert`)
-> Openness to experience (`Open to New Ideas`/`Traditional`)
