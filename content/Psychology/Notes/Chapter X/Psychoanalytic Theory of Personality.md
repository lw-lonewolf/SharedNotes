### Karen Horney
Agreed with Sigmund Freud but disagreed with the Oedipus Complex kinda thing. She said that it was NOT sexual urge but instead basic anxiety that caused such emotions in the child. Child is dependent on the parents for basic needs, once he realizes this he calms down. Three basic strats to deal with shit: 
1) Towards People 
2) Against People 
3) Isolation
