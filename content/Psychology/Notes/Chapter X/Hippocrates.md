Hippocrates presented his theory in Ancient Greece (~2000BC-0AD)
* Ancient Greek philosophers such as Hippocrates classified each types of "humors" in Man. Each type was believed to be because of excessive presence of one of the four bodily fluids that were supposed to constitute the human body. 
-> Earth (Black Bile) `Melancholic` *High Neuroticism* **Depressed**
-> Fire (Yellow Bile) `Choleric` *Agreeableness* **Irritable**
-> Water (Phlegm) `Phlegmatic` *Low Neuroticism* **Calm**
-> Air (Blood) `Sanguine` *Openness to experience* **Optimistic**
