### Expectancy: 
Percieved probability that effort will lead to good performance. This is about what employees expect from their own good performance and outcomes that they will obtain from it. Part of this expectation is the level of difficulty he experiences. i.e. goal difficulty. *An organization can respond by finding what motivates the employee to give best possible performance. These facilities may be training or support.*
### Instrumentality 
Perceived probability that good performance will result in desired outcomes. Each employee is a cog in the organization. Instrumentality can be achieved by the organization providing incentives or rewards for the employees, this process must be transparent so that the employees know that this level of performance would result in rewards, promotions etc. ,

### Valence
Value of expected outcomes to the individual. This values is based on their own basic needs. The organization should research each individual and figure out what motivates them the most. For example certain employees may prefer monetary benefits (bonuses) while other employees may prefer more days off.


# Introduction 
In 1964, Canadian professor of Psychology, *Victor Vroom* from the Yale School of Business put forward the vroom Expectancy theory, his work was based on the work environment. He claimed that when employees have the choice they aim for what motivates them the most. 

He introduced a formula 
$$\text{Motivational Force} = \text{Expectancy} * \text{Instrumentality} * \text{Valence}$$
