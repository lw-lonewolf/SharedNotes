**Origin:** Motivation is derived from the Latin word "movere" which means "to move" or "to energize" or "to activate".

### *Definitions:*
**Motivation:**
* A process that arouses, maintains and guides behavior towards a certain objective.
* It refers to all the internal conditions that stir up activity and sustain activity in order to attain a certain goal.
**Need:** A motivating force that compels action for its satisfaction 
**Drive:** A state of tension and arousal triggered by cues important for survival.
**Incentive:** A reward that pulls on organism's behavior in a particular direction. 
	**Intrinsic:** Gives personal satisfaction to the individual e.g. feedback, acknowledgement.
	**Extrinsic:** A concrete reward received by the individual from external environment, e.g. pay raise, promotion etc. 
**Motive:** 
Psychological state that moves us to engage us in activities that would maximize or move us towards the achievement of a certain goal.
