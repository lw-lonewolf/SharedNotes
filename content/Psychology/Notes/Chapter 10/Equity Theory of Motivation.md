*John Stacey Adams*

The Adams Equity Theory states that inequality in rewards or "favoritism" in an organizational workplace may demotivate other employees or result in a higher employee turn over because they will tend to test their luck at another employer if they feel they are not being treated justly. Fair Treatment of all is necessary and will motivate the workforce to try their best.

According to this theory (Input == Output)? returns true.

##### Input: 
The input referred to by Adams Equity Theory includes both the quantity and quality of the work put in by the employees. The most common forms of input include: 
1) Effort 
2) Skills 
3) Knowledge 
4) Experience 
5) Social Skills 
6) Loyalty 

### Output: 
Output can generally be divided into two further sub categories.
1) Financial or Substantial Awards: 
	 -> Salary 
	 -> Bonus
	 -> Promotion
2) Immaterial Rewards 
    -> Praise 
    -> Recognition
    -> Responsibility
