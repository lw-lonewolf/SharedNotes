The four neurotransmitters we studied are: 
-> Acetylcholine 
-> Dopamine 
-> Serotonin
-> Endorphins 

### AcetylCholine\
-> Every movement that is made- typing, walking, talking, breathing
->ACh also appears to contribute to arousal, attention, and memory
-> An inadequate supply of ACh in certain brain areas causes memory losses as in Alzheimer's disease. 
### Dopamine
-> It is used by neurons that control voluntary movements. 
-> The degeneration of such neurons in a specific in a specific area of the brain causes Parkinson that causes loss of control over voluntary movement. 
-> Rewarding effects of most abused drugs depend on increased activity in a particular dopamine pathway.
-> Drug addiction and different cravings are caused by low amounts of dopamine 
-> Abnormal of amounts of dopamine -> schizophrenia
### Serotonin: 
-> This causes regulation of sleep and eating behavior.
-> Evidence says that serotonin also controls aggression behavior in animals. 
-> Dysregulation in serotonin circuits causes eating disorders.
-> Depressive disorders. 
#### Endorphins
-> Endorphins are internally produced chemicals that resembles opiates in structure and effects. 
-> Contribute to the modulation of pain
