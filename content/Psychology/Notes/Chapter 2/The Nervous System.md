The nervous system is made of up small components called neurons, which are the basic elements of the nervous system. A neuron messages, the dendrites which receive messages from other neurons, and the axon, which carries messages. This axon is protected by the sausage like myelin sheath. The brain and the heart are the only parts of the human body that don't shut down completely. To prevent messages from short-circuiting one another the myelin sheath exists.

##### Components of a Neuron: 
`Dendrites:` The part of a neuron that play a role in receiving information. 
`Axon:` A long thin fiber used by neurons for communicating with each other. 
`Soma:` Cell Body, contains the nucleus and other chemical machinery common to cells 
`Terminal Button:` Are small knobs that secrete chemicals called neurotransmitters
`Synapse:` A junction where information is transmitted from one neuron to another
