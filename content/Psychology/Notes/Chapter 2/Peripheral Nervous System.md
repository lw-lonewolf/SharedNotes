### Sympathetic Division
-> Controls automatic, involuntary, visceral functions that people can't control
-> heart rate, digestion, perspiration
-> mediates much of the physiological arousal 
-> flight-or-fight response 
-> Acts to prepare body in a stressful emergency situation, engaging resources to respond to threat. 

### Parasympathetic Division
-> Conserves bodily resources 
-> Activates processes that allow to body to conserve and store energy 
-> Actions by parasympathetic reduce heart rate, reduce blood pressure, promotes digestion.
