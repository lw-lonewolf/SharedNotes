### BRAIN: 
### HIND BRAIN 
-> circulating blood, breathing, regulating reflexes like coughing, sneezing, and salivating
-> sleep and arousal 
-> coordination of movement and maintaining physical balances 
-> fine motor skills like writing, typing, playing musical instrument

### MID BRAIN
-> vision and hearing
-> dopamine releasing neurons
-> modulation of muscle reflexes, breathing and pain management
-> regulation of sleep and arousal 

### FORE BRAIN 
-> largest most complex part of brain 
-> all sensory information must pass through it. 
-> regulator of biological needs, hunger, thirst, sexual motivation, and temperature regulation
-> one such function ->controlling the autonomic nervous system 
-> vital link between brain and endocrine nervous system
-> basic bio drives including the so-called "four F's": fighting, fleeing, feeding, and mating. 
->emotion, memory, and motivation
->central role in the learning of fear responses 
-> Pleasure centers. 
-> learning, remembering, thinking. 
-> control over fingers lips tongues
-> acquiring new motor skills 
-> empathy, understanding others intentions. 
-> reasoning about relations between objects and events
-> decision making
-> executive control system
