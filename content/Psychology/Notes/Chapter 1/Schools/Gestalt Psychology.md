This was another school of Psych that emerged in the early 20th Century focusing on human perception and experience. The term "Gestalt" is a german word "form" or "configuration" and the theory emphasizes on how humans naturally organize their perceptions into coherent wholes instead of perceiving individual elements in isolation.

###### Key Figures: 
->Max Wertheimer
->Kurtz Kafka
->Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

###### Perception and Patterns:
 * Gestalt Psychology emphasizes that the brain does not perceive individual sensory inputs, instead it combines them to form "patterns" or "wholes", which are formed from relations and structures within the input.
 * For example when looking at a face, we don't see the individual features, instead we see the face as a whole, similar to how we see art instead of each lines/stroke that it's made of. 
 * This differs from other schools of psychology as this is the only holistic school that focuses on the collective experience of the individual instead of breaking down behavior or mental processes. It argues that to understand human perception, behavior or thought, we must look at the *whole pattern* of sensory activity and perception.
