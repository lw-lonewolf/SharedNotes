**William James**

This was developed by William James, it is an early approach to psychology that concerned with that mind does, the mental functions of each activity, and the role of behavior in allowing people to adapt to their environment.
##### Differences between Structuralism and Functionalism: 
S: It is based on notion that task of psychology is to analyze consciousness into its basic elements and investigate how its elements are related. Methods of introspection - careful, systematic self observation of one's own conscious experience.

F: This focused on the belief that psychology should investigate the function or purpose of consciousness, rather than its structure. This was impressed by Charles Darwin. William James -> Not what mind does, but why it does it.
