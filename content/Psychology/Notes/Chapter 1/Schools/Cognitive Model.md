The *cognitive* model of psychology developed by Aaron T. Beck and Albert Ellis, focus on how mental processes influence behavior. It emphasizes that psychological disorders often stem from dysfunctional thought patterns and maladaptive ways of interpreting and responding to experiences. 
#### Cognitive Model: 
The cognitive model focuses on the various mental processes including: 
-> Reasoning and Problem Solving
-> Language
->Creativity, motivation and instincts
->Beliefs
->Memory

It is concerned with how individuals perceive interpret and mentally process their experiences.
##### Active Agents: 
This theory considers its individuals as *active agents* in their thinking. People have different ways of reacting to stimuli. They think, manipulate, interpret and design their environment. In other words, individuals play a direct role in their behavior through thoughts and perceptions. This sets it aside from the other schools as other schools mainly focused on external stimuli.
#### Dysfunctional Thought Processes: 
One key contribution by Beck and Ellis was the idea that many psychological disorders (such as anxiety, depression, and other abnormalities) are caused by dysfunctional or distorted thinking. The absence of sufficient planning and rational thought can also lead to psychological issues.
#### Cognitive Therapy: 
Aron Beck -> CT (Cognitive Therapy) -> identify and challenging negative thought patterns and replacing them with more positive and rational thoughts.

Albert Ellis -> (REBT) *Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy* -> helps individuals recognize irrational beliefs and replace them with more logical and constructive ways of thinking.
