*Sigmund Freud*

Sigmund Freud introduced and developed psychoanalysis, a method for treating psychological problems of people by bringing thoughts/memories/desires suppressed in the subconscious part of the brain to the active part of it. Psychodynamic theory focuses on the influence of the unconscious mind on behavior.
###### Psychoanalysis: 
Freud's innovative treatment, called psychoanalysis, aimed to help patients with repressed or unconscious material to the surface of awareness. Through techniques called *free association*, *dream analysis*, and *talk therapy*. Freud sought to explore the unconscious and its influence on the thoughts actions and behaviors of people. 

###### Unconscious Mind
* According to Freud, the unconscious mind contains thoughts, memories, and desires that are buried below the level of conscious awareness but still exert a powerful effect on behavior. These are often the result from a suppressed or upset childhood.
* For example, traumatic experiences, unresolved desires,or internal conflicts are often pushed into the unconscious, but they continue to shape an individual's personality, habits and emotional responses.
###### Psychodynamic Theory:
Freud's Psychodynamic Theory explains personality, motivation, and mental disorders based on the role of the subconscious mind in all of this. He proposed that many behaviors, especially those related to mental health, are the result of hidden unresolved internal conflicts stored in the unconscious. 

This theory also introduces the concepts of *id*, *ego*, *superego*
*id* -> The instinctive, primitive part of the mind, driven by desires and the need for immediate satisfaction. 
*ego* -> The rational part that acts as a mediator between the *ids* desires and reality, helping a person make rational decisions 
*superego* -> The moral component that internalizes societal rules and norms, acting as a conscience.

`In summary, Freud’s psychodynamic theory emphasizes the unconscious mind and its influence on human behavior. Through psychoanalysis, Freud aimed to uncover hidden conflicts and desires, explaining how these unconscious factors shape personality, motivation, and mental health.`
