According to wundt, Psychology was to be a science, just like physics, chemistry etc. with the *consciousness* as the primary focus. Thus psychology became the "scientific study of conscious experience". Wundt was a tireless scholar who produced more than 54, 000 pages of books and articles during his career. Studies in his lab (in Leipzig University) focused on attention, memory, various reaction-time experiments that provided estimates of the duration of various mental processes. Outstanding young scholars including many Americans came to Germany to study under Wundt, and indeed these scholars fanned out and started labs of their own in various universities. It was in North America, that wundt's new science grew by leaps and bounds. Between 1883 and 1893, some 23 new psych labs sprang up in the US + CAN, many of which were started by his students or by his student's students.
