`Normality` The WHO considers normality to be a state of complete mental, physical and social well being. 
`Abnormality` It is defined as behavior that causes people to experience distress and hinders them in performing daily activities.

##### Causes of PD: 
-> Biological Influences: 
* evolution
* individual genes
* brain structure and chemistry
-> Psychological Influences: 
* stress
* trauma
* learned helplessness
* mood-related perceptions
-> Social-Cultural Influences:
* roles
* expectations
* definitions of normality and disorder
