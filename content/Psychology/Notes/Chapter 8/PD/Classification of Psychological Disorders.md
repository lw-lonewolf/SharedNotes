There are two main categories of Psychological Disorders 
`Neurosis` and `Psychosis`
`Neurosis` are minor mental setbacks, that influence but do not alter perception/are not serious enough to be a threat. 
`Psychosis` are major mental setbacks, that may result in severe damage to self or others if not properly diagnosed and dealt with.

##### Personality Disorders
These are also known as Character disorders. They are categorized into three main clusters. 
###### Cluster A (odd/eccentric)
`Paranoid Personality Disorder:` Characterized by irrational suspicions and mistrust of others. 
`Schizoid Personality Disorder:` Lack of Interest in social relationships, seeing no point in sharing time with others.
`Schizotypal personality disorder:` also avoids social relationship, but due to fear of people

###### Cluster B (dramatic/erratic/emotional)
`Antisocial Personality Disorder:` Pervasive Disregard for the law and rights of others. 
`Borderline Personality Disorder:` Extreme 'Black and White' thinking, instability in relationships, self-image, identity and behavior.
`Histrionic Personality Disorder:` Pervasive Attention-Seeking Behavior including inappropriate sexual seductiveness and shallow or exaggerated emotions. 
`Narcisstic Personality Disorder:` A pervasive pattern of grandiosity, need for admiration, lack of empathy.

###### Cluster C (anxious/fearful)
`Avoidant Personality Disorder:` Social Inhibition, feeling of inadequacy, extremely sensitive to negative evaluation and avoids social interactions 
`Dependant Personality Disorder:` Pervasive psychological dependence on other people. 
`Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder` (NOT SAME AS OCD): rigid conformity to rules, moral codes and excessive orderliness.
