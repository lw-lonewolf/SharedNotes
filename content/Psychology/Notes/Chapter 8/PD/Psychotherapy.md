Therapies for Psychological Disorders

> Psychodynamic

 * Psychoanalysis
 * Brief Psychodynamic therapy

> Humanistic

* Client-centered Therapy
* Gestalt Therapy

> Cognitive

* Rational-Emotive Therapy
* Cognitive Therapy
* Mindfulness based Cognitive-Behavioral Treatments

> Behavioral 
* Classical Conditioning 
  * Exposure
  * Systematic Desensitization
  * Aversion Therapy
* Operant Conditioning
  * Positive reinforcement
  * Punishment
* Modelling
  * Social Skills Training

> Biological

* Drug Therapy
* Electro-convulsive Therapy
* Psycho-surgery


```
 Psychodynamic: Explores unconscious conflicts and past experiences to
  understand current problems; treats depression, anxiety, personality
  disorders.

  Psychoanalysis:  A long-term psychodynamic therapy involving free association and dream analysis; treats various neuroses and personality disorders.

  Brief Psychodynamic Therapy: A shorter-term, focused psychodynamic approach; treats depression, anxiety, relationship problems.

  Humanistic: Emphasizes personal growth, self-acceptance, and free will;
  treats low self-esteem, anxiety, and mild depression.

  Client-centered Therapy:  Provides unconditional positive regard and empathy to facilitate self-discovery; treats anxiety, depression, and relationship issues.

  Gestalt Therapy: Focuses on present awareness and integrating conflicting
  parts of the self; treats anxiety, depression, and relationship problems.

  Cognitive: Identifies and challenges negative thought patterns to change
  behaviors and emotions; treats depression, anxiety, OCD.

  Rational-Emotive Therapy (RET):  Identifies and disputes irrational beliefs;
  treats anxiety, depression, and anger management issues.

  Cognitive Therapy:  Focuses on identifying and modifying maladaptive thought
  patterns; treats depression, anxiety, and eating disorders.

  Mindfulness-Based Cognitive-Behavioral Treatments (MBCT): Combines mindfulness
  techniques with CBT to reduce relapse in depression and anxiety.

  Behavioral: Uses learning principles to modify maladaptive behaviors; treats
  phobias, anxiety, and addictive behaviors.

  Classical Conditioning Therapies (Exposure, Systematic Desensitization,
  Aversion Therapy):  Associate stimuli with different responses to change
  behavior; treats phobias, anxiety, addictions.

  Exposure Therapy:  Gradually exposes individuals to feared stimuli to reduce
  anxiety; treats phobias, PTSD, anxiety disorders.

  Systematic Desensitization:  Pairs relaxation techniques with gradually
  increasing exposure to feared stimuli; treats phobias and anxiety.

  Aversion Therapy: Pairs undesirable behavior with unpleasant stimuli to
  reduce its frequency; treats addiction and paraphilias (controversial).

  Operant Conditioning (Positive Reinforcement, Punishment): Uses rewards and punishments to shape behavior; treats ADHD, autism, and behavioral problems.

  Modeling (Social Skills Training):  Observational learning to acquire new
  behaviors; treats social anxiety, autism, and communication disorders.

  Biological: Uses medical interventions to treat psychological disorders;
  treats severe depression, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder.

  Drug Therapy: Uses medications to alter brain chemistry and alleviate
  symptoms; treats depression, anxiety, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder.

  Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT):  Uses brief electrical stimulation to the
  brain to treat severe depression; treats severe depression when other
  treatments fail.

  Psychosurgery:  Brain surgery to alter brain structures and treat severe
  mental illness; treats severe OCD, depression (rarely used).
```
