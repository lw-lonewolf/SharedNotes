#### The Diathesis-Stress Model 

*Diathesis:* A predisposition or Vulnerability (Usually inherited to develop the disorder)

*Stress:* Environmental Stressors (Prenatal Trauma, Childhood Sexual Abuse, Family Conflict etc.)

*Development of the Disorder:* The stronger the diathesis, the less stress is necessary to produce disorder

Diathesis + Stress -> Development of Disorder
