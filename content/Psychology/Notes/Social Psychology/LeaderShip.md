A process whereby an individual influences a group or individuals to achieve a common goal. 
`Professor John Halley`

Leadership is the process of motivating other people to act in particular ways in order to achieve a certain goal. 
`Tim Hannagan`

...whatever disagreements there are about the precise definition of the term. It is indubitably about people and the extent to which they can be motivated to behave in a particular way. 
`Rose and Lawton`

Leadership is a social influence. It means leaving a mark. It is initiating and guiding and the result is change.
`Manning and Curtis`
