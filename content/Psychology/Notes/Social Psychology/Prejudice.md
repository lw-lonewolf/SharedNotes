The negative attitude held towards members of a group. 
may lead to `discrimination` which involves varied/harsher/unfair behavior towards member of that group. 

Like other attitudes, has three components 
1) Beliefs are cognitive comp. based on thinking. (*All Indians are alcoholics*)
2) Emotions are affective components based on feelings. (*e.g. I hate jews*)
3) Behavioral dispositions are objective and observable in nature (*e.g. I wont hire a indian/mexican/jew*)

#### Factors that lead to Prejudice 
##### Stereotyping
Judging someone based on the basis of our perception of the group which he or she belongs to. (Introvert he ye? He doesn't care for anyone)
##### Subjectivity
People will see what they WANT to see in others. 
##### Memory Bias
someone believes 'women are not cut out for leadership roles', will forget accomplishments but hang on to the short comings.
##### Forming & preserving Prejudicial Attitudes
A legacy from parents. Learned from them or are societal.
##### Making Biased Attribution
Assuming other's behavior reflects their dispositions, can contribute to prejudice. 

#### Elimination of Prejudice
##### Evaluating Credibility
-> Should understand that credibility and bias are central to determining the quality of information and looks at factors such as vested interests, credentials, appropriate expertise. 

##### Recognizing Social Influence Strategies
Is aware of: 
* *Foot in the Door Technique*: Get People to agree to something smaller in the hopes to get them to agree to a larger request later on.
* *Low Ball Technique:* Involves getting someone to commit to seemingly attractive preposition before hidden costs are revealed. 
* *Reciprocity Norm:* The rule that we should pay back in kind what we receive from others.
