Study of Groups. Also a general term for group processes. 

`A group is two or more individuals who are connected to each other by social relationships. Because they interact and influence each other, groups develop a number of dynamic processes that seperate them from a random collection of individuals`

These may include `norms` `roles` `relations` `development` `need to belong` `social influence` `effects on behavior`.

Groups can be classified as *aggregate*, *primary*, *secondary* and category groups. 

#### Group Structures
-> Group Size 
-> Group Roles 
-> Group Norms
-> Group Cohesiveness


There are three levels of `SOCIAL INFLUENCE`: 
1) _highest_ -> obedience -> do as others <u>command</u>.
2) *mid* -> compliance -> do as others <u>want</u>
3) *low* -> conformity -> do as others <u>do</u>

#### Difference between Conformity and Obedience
##### Conformity
 -> Adjusting behavior and thinking in order to match a group 
 standard. Behavior is *contagious*, we follow it to <u>fit in</u>
##### Obedience 
Other behaviors may be an expression of compliance (obedience) towards authority in the group.
