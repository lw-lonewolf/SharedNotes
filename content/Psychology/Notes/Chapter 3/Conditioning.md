Conditioning involves *learning associations between events* that occurs in an organisms environment. 

It is further divided into two parts: 
1) Classical Conditioning (Ivan Pavlov)
2) Operant Conditioning (B.F. Skinner)

### Classical Conditioning
This suggest that learning occurs when an association is formed between a previously neutral stimulus and a naturally occurring stimulus. 

### Operant Conditioning
A type of associative learning that involves strengthening or weakening a behavior by using reinforcement or punishment
