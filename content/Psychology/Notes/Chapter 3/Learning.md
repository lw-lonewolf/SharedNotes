Learning is any relatively durable change in behavior or knowledge that is due to experience. 

### Types of Learning: 
-> Trial and Error (Thorndike)
-> Insight Learning (Wolfgang Kohler)
-> Learning by Imitation or Observational Learning (Albert Bandura)
-> Cognitive Learning (E.C. Tolman)
-> Conditioning 
	-> Classical Conditioning (Ivan Pavlov)
	-> Operant Conditioning (B.F Skinner)
