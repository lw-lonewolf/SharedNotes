A cognitive form of learning involving the mental rearrangement or restructuring of the elements in a problem  to achieve a sudden understanding of the problem and arrive at a solution. Insight learning -> Kohler -> Ape experiment. 

This involves seeing the problem in a new and often unique way by reorganizing the mental representation of the problem.

### Historical Context: Wolfgang Köhler and the Gestalt School of Psychology

Insight learning was first studied in depth by **Wolfgang Köhler**, one of the founders of Gestalt psychology. In the early 20th century, Köhler conducted experiments with chimpanzees to observe their problem-solving abilities.

- **Köhler’s Experiments**: In one famous experiment, Köhler placed a banana out of reach of aone chimpanzee named Sultan, along with some tools like sticks or crates. Initially, Sultan tried different ways to reach the banana but then stopped. After some time, Sultan suddenly realized that he could use a stick to retrieve the banana, indicating that the solution had come to him through insight, rather than through gradual learning.
