#### Functional Requirements: 
Describe: Interaction between the system and its environment, how should the system behave given certain stimuli, required behaviour in terms of required activities. 

*Define* the scope of the product. 
*Describe* the things the system must do and the data manipulated by the functions 

#### Non Functional Requirements 

Describe Quality Characteristics -> System must possess.
Provides: Look/Feel Requirements, Usability Requirements, Operational environment, security requirements, etc. 


#### Measurements that are related to Requirements: 
* Number of requirements can give us a sense of the size of the system. 
* Number of changes to requirements, many changes indicate instability or uncertainty in our understanding of the system.
* Requirements size and change measurements, should be recorded by requirements type. 
* Numbering structure depth metric help measure the appropriateness of level of detail. 
* Measuring writing quality of requirements.
	* Should be rated by the users (the developers, etc.)
	* Should be rated from 1 - 5 (check rating scheme in slides)
