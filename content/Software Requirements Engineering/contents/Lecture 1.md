Define the problem that requires solution. Derive, validate, and  maintain requirements. When defining the requirements, you ask questions you make sure they're correct and then maintain them.

We use *measurements* that's why its called Software Engineering. If something in the process is *measured to be beneficial* for it we use it again, and if its not then we don't. Responsibilities of a software engineer include giving the most quality while keeping time and budget in perspective.

The ability to decompose a problem into smaller sub problems and then creating solutions for them and then synthesizing all of these to create the solution for the original problem. **Communication:** 1) Comprehension 2) Expression 
Comprehension: Understanding what the stakeholders want 
Expression: Expressing what we mean
