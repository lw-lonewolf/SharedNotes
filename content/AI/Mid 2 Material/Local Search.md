In many optimization problems, the path to the goal doesn't matter; the goal state itself is the solution. 

i.e. **state space** consists of complete configurations instead of partial parts.

For such problems we use ***local search algorithms***. They maintain a **<u>single current state</u>** and <u>iteratively</u> improve it.

### Advantages
* Very small memory usage
* Find reasonable solution in infinite/continous state space for which asymmetric solutions are not possible.
