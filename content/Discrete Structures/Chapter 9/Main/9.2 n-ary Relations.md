Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3},...A_{n}$ be sets. A n-ary relation on these sets is a subset of the following $A_{1} \times A_{2} \times A_{3} \quad ,..., \times A_{n}$. 
$A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3},...A_{n}$ are known as domains. and $n$ is called the degree (total number of columns) .
