A relation on a set $A$ which is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive at the same time is known as an equivalence relation. 

>Two elements that are related by an equivalence relation are called equivalent. They are written as $A \sim B$ is often used to denote that A and B are equivalent elements with respect to a particular equivalence relation.
