Let $R$ be a relation of degree $m$ and S is a relation of degree $n$ . The join $J_{p}(R,S)$
where $(p \le m) \land (p \le n)$, is a relation of degree $(m+n-p)$ that consists of all n-tuples $(a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3} ,..., a_{m-p}, c_{1}, c_{2}, c_{3}) \in R$ and $(c_{1}, c_{2}, c_{3}, b_{1}, b_{2},\dots,b_{n-p}) \in S$.
