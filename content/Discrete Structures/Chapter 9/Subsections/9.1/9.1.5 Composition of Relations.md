$S \circ R$

$$(\forall (a,b) \in R) \land (\forall (b,c) \in S) \implies (c,a) \in S \circ R, \quad a \in A, \, b \in B, \, c \in C $$
Powers: 
$R^{1}, R^{2}, R^{3}$
$R^1 = R$
$R^2 = R \circ R$
$R^{3} = (R \circ R) \circ R) \quad \text{or} \quad R^{2} \circ R$
$R^4 = R^3 \circ R$
also, 
$R^{n} = R^{3} , n = 5,6,7,....$

### REPRESENTATION
$S\circ R$ = $M_{S} \times M_{R}$


$S =\quad \begin{array}{ccc} a_{1} & b_{1} & c_{1}\\  d_{1} & e_{1} &f_{1} \\ g_{1}& h_{1}& i_{1}\end{array}$

$R =\quad \begin{array}{ccc} a_{2} & b_{2} &c_{2} \\  d_{2} & e_{2} &f_{2} \\ g_{2}& h_{2}& i_{2}\end{array}$


$S \circ R = \begin{array}{ccc}((a_{1} \land a_{2}) \lor (b_{1} \land d_{2}) \lor (c_{1} \land g_{2})) & ((a_{1} \land b_{2}) \lor (b_{1} \land c_{2}) \lor (c_{1} \land h_{2})) & ((a_{1} \land c_{2}) \lor (b_{1} \land f_{2}) \lor (c_{1} \land i_{2})) \\((d_{1} \land a_{2}) \lor (e_{1} \land d_{2}) \lor (f_{1} \land g_{2})) & ((d_{1} \land b_{2}) \lor (e_{1} \land c_{2}) \lor (f_{1} \land h_{2})) & ((d_{1} \land c_{2}) \lor (e_{1} \land f_{2}) \lor (f_{1} \land i_{2})) \\((g_{1} \land a_{2}) \lor (h_{1} \land d_{2}) \lor (i_{1} \land g_{2})) & ((g_{1} \land b_{2}) \lor (h_{1} \land c_{2}) \lor (i_{1} \land h_{2})) & ((g_{1} \land c_{2}) \lor (h_{1} \land f_{2}) \lor (i_{1} \land i_{2}))\end{array}$
