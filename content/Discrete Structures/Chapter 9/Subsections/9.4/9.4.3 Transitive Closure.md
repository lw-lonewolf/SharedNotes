Let $R$ be a relation on a set $A$. The connectivity relation $R*$ consists of the pairs $(a, b)$ such
that there is a path of length at least one from $a$ to $b$ in $R$. Then the transitive closure of this relation this connectivity relation $R*$. 

In matrix form $M_{R*} = M_{R} \lor M_{R}^2 \lor M_{R}^3$
