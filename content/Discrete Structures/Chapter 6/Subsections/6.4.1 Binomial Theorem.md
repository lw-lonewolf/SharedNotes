Let x and y be variables, and let n be a nonnegative integer.
$$
( x+y )^{n}=\sum_{j=0}^{n} \binom{n} {j} x^{n-j} y^{j}=\binom{n} {0} x^{n}+\binom{n} {1} x^{n-1} y+\cdots+\binom{n} {n-1} x y^{n-1}+\binom{n} {n} y^{n}. 
$$

#### Corollary 1: 
$\sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n} {k}=2^{n}$
(Note to Self: for combinatorial proof, remember that $2^n$ represents total subsets of a set)
#### Corollary 2: 
$\sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1 )^{k} \binom{n} {k}=0$ 

Remark: Corollary 2 implies that
$$
\binom{n} {0}+\binom{n} {2}+\binom{n} {4}+\cdots=\binom{n} {1}+\binom{n} {3}+\binom{n} {5}+\cdots. 
$$
#### Corollary 3: 
Let $n$ be a non-negative integer. Then
$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} 2^{k} \binom{n} {k}=3^{n}. 
$$
