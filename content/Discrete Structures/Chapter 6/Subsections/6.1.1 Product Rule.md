Suppose that a problem can be broken down into a sequence of two tasks. If there are are $n_{1}$ ways to solve the first task and for each of these ways of doing this first task there are $n_{2}$ ways of doing the second task, then there are $n_{1}n_{2}$ ways of doing that procedure. 

#### In Set Form: 
Here the form -> $|A|$ means number of elements of A.
$|A_{1} \times A_{2} \times \dots \times A_{m}| = |A_{1}| \times |A_{2}| \times \dots \times |A_{m}|$
