An $r$-combination of elements of a set is an unordered selection of $r$ elements from the set.
The number of $r$-combinations of a set with n elements, where $n$ is a non negative and $r$ is an integer where $0\leq r\leq n$, equals: 

> $C(n,r) = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$

> Let $n$ and $r$ be non-negative integers with $r \leq n$. Then $C(n,r) = C(n, n-r)$
