 A permutation of a set of distinct objects is an ordered arrangement of these objects. We are also interested in ordered arrangements of some of the elements of a set. An ordered arrangement of $r$ elements is called $r$-permutation 

> If $n$ is a positive integer and $r$ is an integer $1 \leq r \leq n$ there there are, $P(n,r) = n(n-1)(n-2)\dots(n-r+1)$ r-permutations of a set with n distinct elements 


> If $n$ and $r$ are integers with $0 \leq r \leq n$ then $P(n,r) = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}$
