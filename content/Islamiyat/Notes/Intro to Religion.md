#### Etymology: 

###### Literal Meaning: 
Religion is derived from the french word `religiun` which means "respect for what is sacred" and the Latin word `Religionem` which means "reverence for the Gods". 
In Urdu we use the word `مضہب` which is originally an Arabic word which means "a way to go", "a path to walk". The word more commonly used in Arabic, and in the Qur'an is `دین` which means "recompense", "commandment" and judgement etc. 
###### Contextual Meaning: 
Idiomatically, religion is the recognition and obedience of a higher unseen power. Words like creed, cult or faith are synonymous to Religion. 

###### Word Religion in Qur'an: 
The most appropriate of the above words in the sense of religion is "deen". Although the Qur'an uses some other words as well in this sense like `ملہ`, `صراط` etc. but the word preferred and used most often in the divine guidance i.e. Qur'an and Hadith is Deen as it signifies the recompense of the actions undertaken in the world. The word `مزہب` has not been used anywhere in the holy scriptures. It is however used in Arabic as "Point of View" or "School of Thought".
