#### Status in the Quran 
*"If you should love Allah, then follow me[Muhammad], [so] Allah will love you and forgive your sins, And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."*

"*But no, by your lord, they will not truly believe until they make you, judge concerning that over which they dispute among themselves and then find within themselves no disconcert from what you have judged between them and submit in submission*"

"*And we have not sent you, [O, Muhammad], save as a mercy to the worlds.*"

"*Allah and his angels send blessing on the Prophet; O you who believe, send blessings on the Prophet and salute him with respect.*"

"*O you who believe, do not put yourselves before Allah and his Prophet but fear Allah. Allah is indeed all knowing and All hearing. O you who believe, do not raise your voices above the voice of the prophet or be loud to him in speech like the loudness of some of you to others, lest your deeds become worthless while your perceive or not.*"

###### Key Points: 
* **Allah and his angels, send blessings.**
* **Sent Muhammad(pbuh) as a blessing for the worlds**
* If you love Allah, then follow me and he shall love you
* They wont be true believers, until they make you judge, and are satisfied with what you decide 
* Do not raise your voice.

#### Status in the light of Ahadith
* By him in Whose Hands is my life, none of you will have faith till he loves me more than his father and children. 
 
 At the Day of Judgement (people will go to other prophets but 
 all of them will say that we are afraid about what will happen to us.) Today only one man can intercede in the court of Allah the Almighty. Go to Prophet Muhammad(pbuh).
 People will come to him, and he will prostrate himself before Allah's Throne. Allah will reply "O Muhammad! Raise your head; intercede, for your intercession will be accepted, and ask(for anything), for you will be given."
* *Question of the Grave*
  Two angels come-> "*What did you use to say about this man (i.e. Muhammad)*" Faithful believer will say I testify that he is Allah's slave and Apostle. Then they will say to him, look at your place in the Hell Fire, Allah has given you a place in Paradise instead of it. So he will see both places.
	On the other hand a hypocrite will reply "I do not know; but I used to say what the people used to say.". He will be replied "Neither did you know nor did you take the guidance (from the Holy Quran)" Then he will be hit with iron hammers once, that he will send such a cry that is heard by everything near him, except Jinns and Human Beings.
