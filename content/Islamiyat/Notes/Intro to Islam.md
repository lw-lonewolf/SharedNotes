# FORE FACE OF ISLAM 
#### Hadith-e-Jibreel: 

Umar reports -> Jibreel came to see the prophet in the shape of a man while some of the companions were sitting around him in the Holy mosque of Madinah, he asked a five questions from the Holy Prophet. The very first was: "Oh Muhammad Tell me about Islam".  He replied: "Islam is that you bear witness that there is no one worthy of worship except Allah and Muhammad is the messenger of Allah, and you establish prayers, and you pay Charity, and you keep the fasts of Ramadan, and you perform Hajj of the House if you can manage the way to it."

### Pillars of Islam 
"Islam is constructed upon five (pillars), bearing the witness that there is no one worthy of worship (God) except Allah, and Muhammad is a slave to Him and His Messenger, establishing Prayers (Salah), paying Charity (Zakat), performing pilgrimage (Hajj) and keeping Fasts of Ramadan (Saum)."
