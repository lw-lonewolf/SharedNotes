#### Core Steps of The Process
* Create Problem and Vision Statements
* Explore and Brainstorm 
* Identify Persona Expectations 
* Construct Context Scenarios
* Identify Design Requirements
#### Categories of Design Requirements
* **Data Requirements:** Involve relationships or dependencies between sets of objects within the system. e.g. Photos, Customer records. 
* **Functional Requirements:** Define operations on data requirements and how they're represented in the interface. 
* **Contextual Requirements:** These address the environment in which the product will be used. e.g. if a phone app needs to be visible in bright light or used with one hand, this is a contextual requirement.
