## Implementation Model
This is how the product actually works under the hood. In software, this means algorithms and code and not physical moving parts. It's the complex reality a field expert knows about.

## Mental Model 
This is the users *internal* understanding of how the product works. People create these simplified models to help them use complex things. For example, you might think of electricity flowing like water through a cord.

## Represented Model
This is how the designer chooses to represent the products functioning to the user. It's the "face" the product puts on the technology. e.g. operating systems can make a network server look like a local disk on your computer, even though actual hardware may be miles away. 

>[!important] THE GOAL
The most important goal for a designer is to make the *Represented Model* as close to user's mental model as possible
