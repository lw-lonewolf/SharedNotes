**Scenario:**  
Your company is designing a **smart home dashboard** that allows users to monitor and control home energy usage from their phone. The product should appeal to both tech-savvy users and environmentally conscious families.

**Questions:**  
a) Identify and briefly describe **three personas** for this system. Mention their **goals** and **motivations**.  
b) Using **Goal-Directed Design**, describe how personas would guide **design decisions** for the dashboard layout and interaction.  
c) Write one **key scenario** thatf shows how a user interacts with the dashboard to reduce monthly energy costs.  
d) Illustrate how this scenario could be transformed into a **use case** and then into a **design requirement**.  
e) Sketch (conceptually describe) the **interaction framework** for viewing daily energy consumption — include the main screens or states involved.

#### (A) 
Ali, 39 years old, Farmer (daily user): A full time farmer who recently became conscious about the environment. Goal: save electricity and monitor consumption. Frustration: complicated and detailed interfaces, no tooltips. 

Rashid, 25 years old, Tech Enthusiast (daily user): Rashid installs and manages smart devices at his home by himself. Goal: improve productivity, attain a central management system for all his smart devices and monitor energy usage on a per-device basis. Frustrations: 
inability to connect devices to the dashboard, slow navigation.

Nabiha, 32, Housewife (daily user): Nabiha picks her children up daily but often forgets to turn off electronic appliances. Goal: control electronic devices remotely. Frustrations: Cluttered and Confusing Interfaces, inability to "favorite" appliances for quick access.


**Primary Persona — Ali, 39, Farmer (Daily User)**

- **Role:** Full-time farmer who recently became conscious about sustainable living and energy use.
    
- **Motivation:** Reduce unnecessary electricity usage and lower bills to support an eco-friendly lifestyle.
    
- **Goals:**
    
    - Monitor total household energy consumption easily.
        
    - Identify high-usage appliances.
        
    - Save costs through better energy planning.
        
- **Frustrations:** Complicated dashboards, lack of tooltips or guidance, too many screens to reach useful stats.

**Secondary Persona — Rashid, 25, Tech Enthusiast (Daily User)**

- **Role:** A tech-savvy homeowner who installs and manages all smart devices himself.
    
- **Motivation:** Achieve total control and visibility across all connected devices.
    
- **Goals:**
    
    - Integrate all devices into one dashboard.
        
    - Monitor energy usage per device and compare performance.
        
    - Automate energy-saving rules for different times of day.
        
- **Frustrations:**
    
    - Difficulty connecting some devices to the dashboard.
        
    - Slow or laggy UI transitions.


**Tertiary Persona — Nabiha, 32, Housewife (Daily User)**

- **Role:** Manages the household and juggles multiple tasks while caring for her children.
    
- **Motivation:** Maintain safety and reduce stress by controlling appliances remotely.
    
- **Goals:**
    
    - Turn off devices remotely when leaving home.
        
    - Quickly access frequently used appliances through “favorites.”
        
    - Receive reminders or alerts when appliances are left on.
        
- **Frustrations:** Cluttered interface, too many options on the main screen, inability to favorite appliances for quick access.
