
Goal-Directed scenarios and Use Cases are both methods of describing a user’s interaction with a system, but they serve very different functions.

| Technique          | Template/Structure                                                               | Primary Focus & Origin                                                                                                                                                                                           | Application                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **User Stories**   | As a `<type of user>`, I want `<some goal>` so that `<some reason>` (or I need). | **Phrased requirements**—they are not actual stories or scenarios, but sentences. Focus is on describing a feature from the desired capability perspective.                                                      | Often followed by a brief description of the necessary interface.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **User Scenarios** | N/A                                                                              | **Broader and more far-reaching cluster of interactions** intended to meet user goals.                                                                                                                           | Focus on the function and presentation of user interfaces and interactions. They do not describe task-level interactions.                                                                                                                              |
| **Use Cases**      | Based on exhaustive descriptions of the system’s functional requirements.        | Focus is on **low-level user action and accompanying system response**. They Doriginate in software engineering rather than interaction design. All possible user interactions are treated as equally important. | Permit a **complete cataloguing of user tasks** for different classes of users. Useful in identifying **edge cases** and determining if a product is functionally complete. They should only be deployed in the **later stages of design validation**. |
