**Personas** are the main characters in the design process and are defined as a **tangible representation of the user** that acts as a believable agent in the setting of a scenario.

- **Focus on Goals:** Personas possess **goals**, which are distinct from simply having tasks.
- **Application:** Personas provide the means to determine _what_ the product should do and _how_ the product should look and behave.
- **Design Starting Point:** The design must start with a story (scenario) describing an ideal experience **from the persona’s perspective**. This perspective focuses on **how the persona thinks and behaves**, without being focused initially on technology or business goals.
- **Derivation:** User goals and needs should be derived from the primary and secondary personas.
