##### Design of Behavior
Design of behavior refers to understanding the user and his relationship with the product from on-boarding all the way to the software's end-of-life, with the most important point being why and how the user wants to use the software. Design of behavior requires greater context knowledge and not just visual representation and brand rules.

##### UX Design 
###### Interaction Design 
Focuses on effort to improve the experience of users by relating behavior to form and content. 
###### Information Architecture
Focuses on what data is provided, how it is provided and its structure.
###### Industrial Design and Graphic Design Focus
Focuses on the form of products and services ensuring form supports use(requiring attention to behavior and content).

#### Division of Responsibilities

* **Engineering Team:** *Implementation and Fabrication*
* **Marketing Team:** *Convincing customers to buy product*
* **Management:** *Profitability of the product and deciding what others work on.*

##### Principles

##### Patterns
Common ways to address user request and design concerns.
##### Processes
How to understand and design user requirements and how to apply design principles and patterns.
