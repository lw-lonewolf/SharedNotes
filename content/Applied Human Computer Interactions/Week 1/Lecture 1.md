###### Human-Computer Interaction
*Human Computer Interaction* is a discipline which is concerned with the design, evaluation and implementation of interactive computing system for human use and the study of major phenomena surrounding them - **Association of Computing and Machinery** (ACM)

###### Interface 
Interface refers to a medium or interaction between the computer and the end user. Other names include *Computer Human Interface(CHI)* or *Man Machine Interaction (MMI)*

##### Objective of HCI
We perform HCI to design systems in such a way that make them efficient, stable, usable and attainable. It is a way to reduce design time through various task models. There are some disciplines contributing to HCI
* Computer Science
* Cognitive Psychology
* Fine Arts Design

##### UI Design vs UX Design vs Interaction Design
*User Experience(UX) is a larger scope that consists of various design and usability disciplines collaborating to create products, systems and of course services. UX Design according to its name is about creating the ideal experience of using a service or a product.*

###### UI vs UX
A *UI* is often part of **UX**, both can be applied to almost any category of product, both refer to solving the problem statement related to "design" aspect of product and both aim to maximize user's comfort.

**UX** covers a persons *behaviors, attitude and emotions* about using a particular product. It includes a person's own perception of system aspects such as *utility, ease of use and efficiency*. This means it may be considered particularly *subjective* in nature. UX is dynamic as it is modified over time due to changing usage circumstances as well as individual systems where they can be found.

*UX* is focused on user journey to solve a problem and the complete experience. 
*Content* is the work's essence or what is being depicted.
