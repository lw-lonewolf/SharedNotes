###### Interaction Design vs User Interface Design
UI Design is normally used to express arrangement of widgets on screen but interaction design goes far beyond. It refers to the way *form*, *function*, *content* and *behavior* are linked together extremely cohesively.

UX has elements like: 
* Storytelling
* Engagement
* Objectives
* Usability
While UI have elements such as: 
* Layout
* Visual Design
* Branding
* Interactive Design

#### Design
Design is a conscious and intuitive effort to impose meaningful order.

* Understanding user desires, needs, motivations and contexts.
* Understanding business, technical and domain opportunities, requirements and constraints.
* Using this knowledge as a foundation for plans to create products whose form, content and behavior is useful, usable and desirable as well as economically viable and technically feasible.
