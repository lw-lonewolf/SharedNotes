
| Concept                    | Description                                                                                                                                 | Distinctions/Caveats                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| :------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Archetype**              | The original pattern or prototype; a perfect example.                                                                                       | Personas must be archetypes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Stereotype**             | A standardized mental picture, oversimplified opinion, prejudiced attitude, or uncritical judgment.                                         | Personas should _not_ be stereotypical. Stereotypes are often used in poor user profiles.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **User Role / Role Model** | An abstraction defined by the relationship between a class of users and their problems (needs, interests, expectations, behavior patterns). | A single role can contain several people who think and act differently (e.g., procurement planners in different industries). Reduces users to a list of attributes (e.g., "car buyer").                                                            |
| **User Profile**           | Often used synonymously with persona if generated from ethnographic data and encapsulating depth.                                           | Often reflects a "brief biographical sketch," consisting of a name, picture, and brief, mostly demographic description. If based on minor demographics (car, kids, income) it is likely based on a stereotype and **not useful as a design tool**. |
| **Market Segment**         | A division of a broad target market into subsets (consumers, businesses) perceived to have common needs, interests, and priorities.         | **Design personas** shed light on product definition/development; **Marketing personas** shed light on the sales process. There is seldom a 1-to-1 mapping of market segments to personas. Personas represent user behaviors, not market segments. |
