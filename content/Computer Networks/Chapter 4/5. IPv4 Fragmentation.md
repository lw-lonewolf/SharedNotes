Depending on MTU(**M**aximum **T**ransmission **U**nit) sizes, we may need to divide a packet into further smaller parts. This is known as IPv4 fragmentation.

Example: 

1600 byte packet that has to cross two routers with mtu 1500b (r1-r2) and 512b (r2-dest).

Now this will be fragmented as: 

## Router 1:

| Total Length | IP Header Length | ID     | DF  | MF  | Fragment Offset |
| ------------ | ---------------- | ------ | --- | --- | --------------- |
| 1500         | 20               | 0xCAFE | 0   | 1   | 0               |
| 120          | 20               | 0xCAFE | 0   | 1   | 185             |
## Router 2: 
| Total Length | IP Header Length | ID     | DF  | MF  | Fragment Offset |
| ------------ | ---------------- | ------ | --- | --- | --------------- |
| 508          | 20               | 0xCAFE | 0   | 1   | 0               |
| 508          | 20               | 0xCAFE | 0   | 1   | 61              |
| 508          | 20               | 0xCAFE | 0   | 1   | 122             |
| 36           | 20               | 0xCAFE | 0   | 1   | 183             |
| 120          | 20               | 0xCAFE | 0   | 0   | 185             |
