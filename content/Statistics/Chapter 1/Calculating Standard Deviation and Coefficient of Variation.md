$X$ = *midpoint of each class*. e.g if class => (5-10) then $X = \frac{5+10}{2}$
$\bar{X}$ = *Arithmetic Mean* = $\frac{\sum FX}{\sum F}$
>[!caution] Calulating $\sum FX$
>$FX = F\cdot X$ and $\sum FX$ is the sum of all $FX$

*Variance* = $\frac{\sum F(X-\bar{X})^2}{\sum F}$
*Standard Deviation* = $\sqrt{ \text{variance} }$
*Coefficient of Variation* = $\frac{\text{Standard Deviation}}{\bar{X}} \cdot 100$



## When we don't have frequency: 
$$\bar{X} = \frac{\sum(X-\bar{X})^2}{n-1}$$
$$\text{Variance} = \frac{\sum(X-\bar{X})^2}{n-1}$$
$$SD = \sqrt{ \frac{\sum(X-\bar{X})^2}{n-1} }$$
$$CV = \frac{SD}{\bar{X}} \cdot 100$$

A standardized variable:
 $$\frac{X-\text{Mean}}{SD}$$
