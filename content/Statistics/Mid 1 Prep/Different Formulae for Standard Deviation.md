### Calculating Variance for a Population
$$\text{Variance} = \frac{\sum{F(X-\bar{X})}}{n-1}$$

### Calculating Variance for a Sample space
$$\text{Variance} = \frac{\sum{F(X-\bar{X})}}{n}$$



### Question for Understanding
- **You have test scores from an entire class of 100 students. Which formula do you use for standard deviation?**
    
    - A) The one with **n** in the denominator (population)
    - B) The one with **n - 1** in the denominator (sample)
- **You surveyed 10 people about their coffee consumption to estimate the coffee habits of your whole university. Which formula do you use?**
    
    - A) The one with **n** in the denominator (population)
    - B) The one with **n - 1** in the denominator (sample)
`Answer : 1) A  2) B`
