## Correlation Coefficient 
It describes the strength of relationship between two sets of interval-scaled or ratio-scaled Variables. Denoted by *r* also called *Pearson's r* or *Pearson's Correlation Coefficient*.  Value ranges from $-1\leq r\leq 1$ , -1 and 1 indicating perfect correlation.

## t-test for Correlation coefficient
Used for testing the significance of the Correlation Coefficient 
$H_{0}: \rho = 0$
$H_1: \rho \neq 0$

Then $t = \frac{{r\sqrt{ n-2 }}}{\sqrt{ 1-r^2 }}$
