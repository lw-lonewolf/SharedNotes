# RIGOR AND FORMALITY 

-> [RIGOR] Defined as precision and exactness (do what ur fucking told to and no more) 

-> FORMALITY is the highest form of rigor. formality means rigor is present but the opposite isn't true.

Software Dev is a creative activity, creative stuff isnt precise or accurate (most of the time). 

Hence rigor is based upon: 
-> formal modeling of reality
-> empirical adjustments that take care of stuff that the model does not deal with directly.
-> rules of thumb based on experience from the past.

The blend of these lead to a rigorous and systematic approach that can be applied time and again. 

> PROGRAMMING IS THE ONLY PHASE OF SOFT DEVELOPMENT WHERE A FORMAL APPROACH IS USED.

Rigor and Fomality should actually be applied throughout the software process.
