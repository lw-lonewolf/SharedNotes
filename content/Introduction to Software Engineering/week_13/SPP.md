## Importance: 
To assess the tasks to perform, the timelines for the work to be conducted, many software engineering tasks must occur in 
parallel to each other. The result of one of these might have a profound effect on the other. these interdependencies are 
difficult to make sense of without creating a schedule.

> WORK PRODUCT: adaptable plan containing
> 1) The Tasks to be performed.
> 2) Functions to be implemented
> 3) the cost, effort and time involved for each. 
> A project schedule is also created based on this info
