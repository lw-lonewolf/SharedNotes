## PLANNING 

Planning requires you to make an initial commitment to a flow of action while accepting the fact that this might not lead to the 
final product. Their always be a certain level of uncertainty in the future of a plan.
 
Whenever estimates are made, their is uncertainty but this does not mean that we can proceed in a haphazard way. We must proceed 
in a systematic way in order to reduce the level of uncertainty.

## ESTIMATION 

Estimation of resources, costs and schedule for software dev. requires: 
-> experience
-> access to good historical info (e.g. process and product metrics) 
-> the courage to commit to quantitative predictions based upon qualitative data (can go really wrong :skull:)

Estimation -> raises risk. Shit like complexity, the fucking size and degree of structure uncertainty all reduce reliability of 
estimates
