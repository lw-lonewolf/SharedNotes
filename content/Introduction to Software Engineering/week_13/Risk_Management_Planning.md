			# RISK MANAGEMENT AMD ANALYSIS 

A risk is a problem that may pop up during the software engineering process and can cause delays. Hence its better to recognize possible risks, analyze them and create a plan to deal with them 


> **THE WORK PRODUCT:  *A RMMM (RISK MITIGATION, MONITORING AND MANAGEMENT) plan or a series of risk info sheets.* **

> **STEPS:**
    
1) risk identification
2) analyis (how much potential damage is probable and what priority does the risk hold (low, medium or high risk) 
3) Development of a plan to manage these risks that have high probability and high impact.    

> **IMPORTANCE:** 
    CAN POTENTIALLY HELP COMPLETE A PROJECT WITH MINIMUM HINDRANCES ALONG THE WAY. A PROJECT without a plan for managing risks can end up in serious trouble which cou;d have been avoided if the project had carried on in a systematic manner and followed its plan.

> **REACTIVE RISK MANAGEMENT:**
    You respond to risks as they occur, relies on contigency plans for mitigiting/reducing impacts. often leads to higher costs. 

> **PROACTIVE RISK MANAGEMENT:**
    Identify and react risks beforeo they occur, obv has less cost and aims to reduce potential disruption 

### REACTIVE: WAIT TILL THERE IS AN ISSUE THEN SOLVE IT. 
### PROACTIVE: ANTICIPATE ISSUE AND SOLVE IT BEFORE IT HAPPENS. 

Types of Software Risks: 
> Project Risks (Schedule Delays, Budget overruns, Poor Project management) 
> Technical Risks (Software Defects, Performance Problems, Sociability issues) 
> Business Risks (Market Changes, Client Withdrawal, Cost-benefit imbalance)
