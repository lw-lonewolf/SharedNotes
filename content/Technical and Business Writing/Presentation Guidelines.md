
* Know your Listeners
* Use the Preachers' Maxim: 1) Get Attention 2) State Exact Purpose 3) List Main Points you will cover. In body cover these points briefly in the same order as the order in introduction. In conclusion review main ideas covered in the body and specify actions that you want. 
* Stick to a few main points. Try to organize in "three" main points if possible 
* Put Outline on Cards or on a Paper: Don't generate on the spot, or memorize stuff, instead prepare an outline for recalling points 
* Practice, Practice, Practice
* Speak Vigorously and Deliberately
* Avoid Filler Words 
* Use Rhetorical Questions
* Maintain Eye Contact
* Use Appropriate Gestures and Posture
