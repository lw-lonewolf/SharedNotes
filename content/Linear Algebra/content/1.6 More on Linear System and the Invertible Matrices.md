### Theorem: 
If `A` is an invertible `n*m` then for each `n*1` matrix b, the system of equations `Ax*b` has exactly one solution. 

###### Example: 
$x_{1} + 2x_{2} + 3x_{3} = 5$ 
$2x_{1} + 5x_{2} + 3x_{2} = 3$
$x_{1} + 8x_{3} = 17$

$\begin{matrix} 1  &2 &3\\ 2 & 5 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 & 8 \end{matrix} * \begin{matrix} x_{1} \\ x_{2} \\ x_{3}\end{matrix} = \begin{matrix} 5 \\ 3 \\ 17\end{matrix}$


#### Linear System of Common Coefficient: 
Ax = b1, Ax2 = b2 ..... Ax = bk is a sequence of linear systems with same coefficients Matrix. This can be solved simultaneously.
