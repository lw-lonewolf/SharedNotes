##### Minor of a Matrix: 
If `A` is a square matrix, then the min
or of entry $a_{ij}$ is denoted by $M_{ij}$ and is defined as determinant of sub-matrix that remains after the `ith` \row and `jth` column are deleted from matrix `A`.

along Column
`det(A)` = $a_{1j}c_{1j} + a_{2j}c_{2j} + \dots + a_{nj}c_{nj}$
along Row:
`det(A)` = $a_{i1}c_{i1} +a_{i2}c_{i2} + \dots + a_{in}c_{in}$

### THEOREM 
If `A` is an `n*n` triangular matrix is upper and lower triangular matrix or then `det(A)` is the product of the enteries on the main diagonal of the matrix (A) = $|a_{11}a_{22}a_{33} \dots a_{nn}|$

### THEOREM\
Let `A` be a square matrix if `A` is a row of zeros or columns of zeros then `det(A) = 0`

### THEOREM
Let `A` be a square matrix then `det(A)` = `det(A^t)`

->If two rows are swapped,  then a negative sign must be introduced.
