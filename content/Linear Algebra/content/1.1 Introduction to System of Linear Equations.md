##### ROW OPERATIONS 
1) Multiply a row through by a nonzero constant 
2) Interchange two rows
3) Add a constant times one row to the other 

### Row Echelon Form (Gauss Elimination Method)
1) If a row does not consist entirely of zeros, then the first non zero number in the row is 1. We call this the leading 1. 
2) If there are any rows that consist entirely of zeroes, then they are grouped together at the bottom of the matrix.
3) In any two successive rows that do not consist entirely of zeros, the leading  1 in the lower row occurs farther to the right than 1 in the higher rows.
### Reduced Echelon Form (Gauss-Jordan Elimination)
1) The matrix is in row-echelon form. 
2) The first non-zero entry in each non-zero row is the non-zero entry in its column.
### Homogeneous Equations: 
Equations where all the constants are zero are called homogeneous equations.
### Heterogeneous Equation
	Where even one of the constant is non-zero.
