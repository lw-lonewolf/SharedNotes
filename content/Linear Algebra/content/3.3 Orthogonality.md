Two vectors are said to be orthogonal if $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 0$

##### Equation of Line and Plane 
###### in 2D
$a(x-x_{o}) + b(y-y_{o}) = 0$
###### in 3D
$a(x-x_{o}) + b(y-y_{o}) - c(z-z_{o}) = 0$

###### Distance between the point and line 
In $R^2$ distance between point $P_{o}(x_{o}, y_{o})$ and line $ax+by+c=0$ is defined as: 
$$D = |\frac{ax_{o}+by_{o}+c}{\sqrt{ a^2+b^2 }}|$$
In $R^3$:
$$D = |\frac{ax_{o}+by_{o}+cz_{o}+d}{\sqrt{ a^2+b^2+c^2 }}|$$
