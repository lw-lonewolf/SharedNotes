#### Norm of a Vector 
If $\vec{v} = (v_{3}, v_{2}, \dots, v_{n})$ is a vector in $R^n$ then norm of vector $\vec{v}$ is denoted by $||V||$ and defined as $||V|| = \sqrt{v_{1}^2 + v_{2}^2 + \dots + v_{n}^n }$

##### Properties of a Norm 
1) $||v|| \geq 0$
2) $||k\vec{v}|| = k||v||$
3) If $||\vec{v}||$ = $0$ if and only if $\vec{v} = \vec{0}$

#### Unit Vector
If $||\vec{u}||  = 1$ it is a unit vector

#### Dot Products using Components
If $\vec{u} = (\vec{u_{1}}, \vec{u_{2}}, \dots \vec{u_{n}})$ and $v = (\vec{v_{1}}, \vec{v_{2}}, \dots \vec{v_{n}}$ then $u \cdot v =u_{1}v_{1} + u_{2}v_{2} + \dots u_{n}v_{n})$
If $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ are non zero vectors in $R^2$ and $R^3$ and $\theta$ is the angle between $u$ and $v$ then the dot product of $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ then the dot product of $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ is denoted by: 
$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}$ = $||u||||v||\cos \theta$
###### Properties: 
1) If $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} > 0$ then $\theta$ is acute angle. 
2) If $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} < 0$ then $\theta$ is obtuse angle.
3) If $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 0$ then $\theta$ is $\frac{\pi}{2}$

#### SPECIAL CASE 
If $\vec{u}= \vec{v}$ then $\vec{v}\vec{v} = v_{1}^2 + v_{2}^2 + \dots + v_{n}^2$ then $\vec{v}\vec{v} = ||\vec{v}||^2$

##### Distance in $R^n$
If $\vec{u} = (\vec{u_{1}}, \vec{u_{2}}, \dots \vec{u_{n}})$ and $v = (\vec{v_{1}}, \vec{v_{2}}, \dots \vec{v_{n}})$ are in $R^n$ then we denote the distance between $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$:
$d(\vec{u}, \vec{v}) = ||\vec{u}-\vec{v}|| = \sqrt{(u_{1}-v_{1})^2 + (u_{2}-v_{2})^2 + \dots + (u_{n}-v_{n})^2}$

##### Cauchy Schinorg Inequality
If $\vec{u} = (\vec{u_{1}}, \vec{u_{2}}, \dots \vec{u_{n}})$ and $v = (\vec{v_{1}}, \vec{v_{2}}, \dots \vec{v_{n}})$ then $|\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}| \leq ||\vec{u}||\cdot||\vec{v}||$


#### Theorem
If $\vec{u}, \vec{v}, \vec{w}$ are vectors in $R^n$ and $k$ is any scalar. 
1) $\vec{0} \cdot \vec{v} = \vec{v} \cdot \vec{0} = \vec{0}$
2) $(\vec{u} + \vec{v}).\vec{w} = \vec{u} \cdot \vec{w} + \vec{v} \cdot \vec{w}$
3) $\vec{u} \cdot (\vec{v} - \vec{w}) = \vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} - \vec{u} \cdot \vec{w}$
4) $(\vec{u} - \vec{v})\cdot\vec{w} = \vec{u} \cdot \vec{w} - \vec{v} \cdot\vec{w}$
5) $k(\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}) = \vec{u}.(k \vec{v})$
#### Theorem
If $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ are vectors in $R^n$ and $k$ is scalar $||\vec{u} + \vec{v}|| \leq ||\vec{u}|| + ||\vec{v}||$

#### Parallelogram Equation for Vectors 
If $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ are vectors in $R^n$ then $||\vec{u} + \vec{v}||^2 + ||\vec{u}-\vec{v}||^2 = 2(||\vec{u}^2|| + ||\vec{v}||^2)$

##### Theorem
If $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ are vectors in $R^n$ with Euclidean Inner product
$$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \frac{1}{4}||\vec{u} + \vec{v}||^2 - \frac{1}{4}||\vec{u} - \vec{v}||^2$$
#### Dot Product as Matrix Multiplication 
1) If $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$  are column matrix $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \vec{u}^t \cdot \vec{v} = \vec{v}^t \cdot \vec{u}$
2) If $\vec{u}$ is a row matrix and $\vec{v}$ is a column matrix then $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = uv = \vec{v}^t \cdot \vec{u}$
3) If $\vec{u}$ is a column matrix and $\vec{v}$ is a row matrix $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \vec{v} \cdot \vec{u} = \vec{u}^t  \cdot \vec{v}^t$
4) If $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ are both row matrix then $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}^t = \vec{v}\cdot\vec{u}^t$
